About Plane GO

Plane GO is a free app for aviation enthusiasts and spotters: it shows the aircraft flying over you in real time, warns you when one is about to become visible, and turns every pass into a chance to learn, photograph and play.

What Plane GO does

  • The Scanner: a radar that shows the aircraft around you on a compass rose, works out when each one will be visible with the naked eye and which way to look, and sends you a notification with a countdown.
  • The Collection: every aircraft photographed while passing joins your collection, with its details (model, airline, route) and rarity. A catalogue of more than 300 models, from the Cessna to Concorde, tracks your progress.
  • The Games: recognise the aircraft flying around you right now, guess where an aircraft will land, which one will pass close to you or which one is flying highest. Right answers earn XP; to climb the ranks you also need to photograph different models and receive likes.
  • The community: a feed of photos shared with friends or with all players, comments, a leaderboard and a photo contest every week.

Where does the information come from?

Aircraft positions come from the community ADS-B receiver networks adsb.fi and adsb.lol, fed by enthusiasts around the world. Aircraft records and routes come from the free adsbdb database, model photos and descriptions from Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons. Maps use public-domain data from Natural Earth. Many thanks to them.

Plane GO is a tool for discovery and leisure: it must never be used for navigation or air safety.

Who are we?

Plane GO is designed and published by David Boccara, an aviation and photography enthusiast, who created it to make spotting accessible to everyone: to those who look up when they hear a plane as well as to photographers with a telephoto lens.

The app is available on the web at plane-go.com, can be installed on the phone's home screen, and as an Android app that keeps the radar running with the screen off.

Our commitments

  • Privacy: your exact position stays on your phone; only a position rounded to about 1 km is used to look for aircraft. Uploaded photos are re-encoded, without their location data. See the Privacy page.
  • Photographing within the rules: every player agrees to take photos only from public places where photography is allowed.
  • Moderation: posts can be reported; after five reports they are removed automatically.
