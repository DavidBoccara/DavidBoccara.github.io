Delete my Plane GO account

You can delete your Plane GO account and all the data linked to it at any time, free of charge. Deletion is permanent.

From the app (immediate)

  1. Open Plane GO (Android app or plane-go.com) and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Delete my account (also at the bottom of the Privacy page).
  4. Confirm by typing SUPPRIMER.

Your account and all your data are deleted immediately, then Plane GO's data is also erased from the phone.

Without the app

If you no longer have access to the app, send a request with the contact form: subject “My personal data”, giving your account's email address and your nickname. We delete the account within 30 days and confirm it to you by email.

Delete part of your data (without deleting your account)

You can erase part of your data yourself at any time and keep your account:

  • A catch and its photos: Collection › Photos, tap the catch then Delete (photos erased from the server and the feed, XP removed).
  • A single photo of an album: in the catch's card, Remove.
  • A comment: on the post, tap the bin next to your comment.
  • Your contest entry: Feed › Contest, Withdraw under your photo.
  • Your posts in the World feed: Profile, untick “World feed” (only your friends see them).

For any other data (profile photo, quiz results, friends list…), ask with the contact form (subject “My personal data”, with your account's email address): it is done within 30 days.

What is deleted with the account

  • Your account: email address, password, nickname, profile photo.
  • Your catches and all their photos (deleted from the server), your XP and your rank.
  • Your friends, likes, comments, quiz results and your entry in the contest of the week.

What may be kept

  • Nothing that identifies you. Only the “winner of the week” mention of a contest you won stays in the contest history, without your name or account.
  • The reports you made on other players' posts are deleted with your account.
